# 🕹️ Arcade Games

A collection of classic and original browser-based games built as a personal portfolio project. All games run directly in the browser — no downloads, no installs, just play.

---

## 🎮 Games

### 2048
Slide numbered tiles across a 4×4 grid and combine matching values to reach the elusive 2048 tile. Simple to learn, hard to put down.

### Chess
A fully functional two-player chess game played in the browser. Classic rules, clean board.

### Connect
The classic Connect Four experience. Drop colored discs into a vertical grid and be the first to line up four in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

### Draw
A freehand drawing canvas where you can sketch, doodle, and paint. Pick your color, grab your brush, and create.

### Ghost Hunting
Nine holes, endless ghosts. Phantoms pop up at random and you have to click them before they vanish — a browser take on the classic whack-a-mole arcade game.

### Gorillas
A turn-based artillery game inspired by the legendary QBasic classic. Two gorillas face off on a city skyline — lob explosive bananas at each other by calculating angle and power.

### Guess
A word-guessing game. You have a limited number of attempts to figure out the hidden word, one letter at a time.

### Memory
A card-flipping memory game. Flip two cards at a time and try to match all pairs in as few moves as possible.

### Piano
An interactive piano keyboard playable via mouse or keyboard. Press the keys and make some noise.

### Pong
The game that started it all. Two paddles, one ball — rally against the computer in this browser take on the arcade legend.

### Pool
A top-down billiards simulator. Line up your cue, adjust your power, and try to pocket all your balls before your opponent does.

### Rocketship
Two rockets, incoming asteroids, one winner. Shoot your opponent's ship while dodging the asteroid field — play solo against the computer or face off against a friend in local multiplayer.

### Secret Number
The computer has a secret number in mind. Type your guess and it will tell you whether the answer is higher or lower — keep narrowing it down until you crack it.

### Snake
Guide the growing snake around the board to eat food without hitting your own tail. A timeless arcade challenge.

### Termo
A Portuguese word-guessing game inspired by Wordle. You get six attempts to guess the hidden five-letter word — each guess reveals which letters are correct, misplaced, or absent.

### Tetris
The iconic falling-block puzzle game. Rotate and place tetrominoes to complete full rows before the stack reaches the top.

### The Cube
An interactive 3D cube you can spin in any direction with your mouse. A satisfying visual experiment built entirely in the browser.

### Tic-Tac-Toe
The classic X's and O's. Take turns marking the grid and try to claim three in a row before your opponent does.

### Town
A block-stacking game where precision matters. Stack each new block as accurately as possible on the one below — any overhang gets trimmed, making every layer a little smaller and the challenge a little harder.

### Traffic Run
Navigate a figure-8 shaped road as traffic builds up around you. Control your car's speed to avoid collisions with other vehicles — the longer you survive, the busier it gets.

### Word Scramble
A jumbled word is presented and you have to unscramble it as fast as you can. Tests your vocabulary and pattern recognition under pressure.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

Built with vanilla JavaScript, [Pug](https://pugjs.org) for HTML templating, and [Stylus](https://stylus-lang.com) for CSS — no UI frameworks, no runtime dependencies.
